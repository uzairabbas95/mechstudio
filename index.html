Our Purpose

Mission Statement

To empower the next generation of innovators, equipping young minds with future-ready skills to design, build, and innovate.

Who We Are

About MechStudio

Our studios are a space that enables kids to be creative, curious and practice critical thinking. We are an innovation lab for young minds equipped with modern tools and workstations to get them started quickly.

Our team of industry professionals in Robotics, Manufacturing, and Engineering offers encouragement, inspiration, and support in a hands-on creative space.

We give students a taste of DIY building and exposure to an innovator's toolbox—equipping them with future-ready skills and leaving them excited to tinker, innovate, and create.

More than a robotics class

Students experience the complete engineering journey—from an idea and digital design to electronics, programming, prototyping, testing, and a working robotic system.

What Students Learn

Four Core Learning Elements

Each element develops practical skills that come together in complete mechatronics and robotics projects.

⚡

Electronics

Circuits, components, sensors, motors, wiring, and the fundamentals behind electronic systems.

</>

Programming

Coding, logic, problem-solving, automation, and controlling real hardware through software.

⬡

CAD & 3D Printing

Designing digital models, creating mechanical parts, and turning ideas into physical prototypes.

🤖

Robotics

Combining mechanical design, electronics, and programming to build, test, and improve working robots.

Learning Pathways

Programs for Young Innovators

Grades 5–7

A practical introduction to engineering through guided projects and age-appropriate technical challenges.

  • Basic circuits and components
  • Introductory programming
  • Simple CAD and 3D printing
  • Beginner robotics projects

Grades 7–9

A deeper engineering experience focused on design thinking, integration, automation, and independent problem-solving.

  • Sensors, motors, and control systems
  • Intermediate programming
  • Mechanical design and prototyping
  • Integrated robotics challenges
